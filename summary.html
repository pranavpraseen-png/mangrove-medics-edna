Every drop has a guest list.

The eDNA Pod is a hand-carried canister that filters rainforest stream water and keeps the DNA animals leave behind, so a lab can tell which species live there.

How it works

  1. Filter the water

    Up to 1 L through a 0.45 µm filter.

  2. Seal and preserve

    ≥95% ethanol on every filter.

  3. Log the details

    GPS, time, turbidity and a QR sample ID.

  4. Lab reads the DNA

    Species named from DNA barcodes.

One trip, one 8-seat carousel

  • Seats 2–7: 6 samples

Why it is different

≈ AED 1,710

in parts for our Pod. Commercial eDNA samplers cost about USD 7,500–11,000.

Blank first

Every trip starts with 1 L of distilled water. A clean blank proves the Pod did not contaminate the samples.

No traps

No cameras either. Carried by hand (about 2.5 kg) to the stream bank; no animal is touched.
