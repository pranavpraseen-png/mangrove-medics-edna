# Canopy Keepers – agreed values (single source of truth)

If any answer file disagrees with this table, this table wins. "approx." = estimate, not measured yet.

| Decision | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Mission layout | Seat 1 = field blank; seats 2–7 = **6 real samples**; seat 8 = empty flush seat | Every mission is checked by a blank; the flush seat cleans the line without wasting a filter |
| Replicates per site | 3 | 2 sites × 3 per mission (or 1 site × 6). Published guidance is 3–6 for high detection |
| Field blanks | 1 per mission (seat 1, 1 L distilled water, forced first by the console) | Proves the shared nozzle and tubing did not contaminate |
| Real samples per mission | 6 (plus 1 blank) | Do not say "8 samples" |
| Volume per filter | Target 1 L. Stop at 1 L, 20 min, or flow below 20 mL/min. A sample counts only if ≥250 mL | 0.45 µm 47 mm filters often clog at 0.25–1 L in muddy water. At 60–100 mL/min, 1 L takes 10–17 min |
| Pump flow (assumed) | approx. 60–100 mL/min with a filter; calibrate by 8 Nov | Small 12 V peristaltic pumps are rated up to about 140 mL/min with no filter |
| Plumbing | Pull-through: intake → cartridge (filter) → pump → waste | Pump only sees filtered water; suction cannot burst tubing |
| Per-site cleaning | New bagged, pre-bleached intake tube + funnel; 10% bleach spray on nozzle for 10 min, rinse; 250 mL site-water flush through seat 8 | 250 mL replaces about 7 mL of tube volume more than 30 times |
| Preservation | ≥95% ethanol (default); Longmire's if the lab asks; ship the liquid with the filter | Guides recommend 95–100%; some DNA leaks into the ethanol |
| Preservation test | Silica gel vs ethanol, paired filters in the aquarium test (6 Dec) | One study found silica better at room temperature |
| Filter | 47 mm, 0.45 µm MCE (Cobetter); cartridge also designed to take 1.2 µm | Most common in eDNA; 1.2 µm backup for muddy water |
| Cartridges | Loaded at school with a new sterile filter, used once per mission, bleached (10 min, no long soak) and reused | Cheap; bleach test on 20 cycles by 8 Nov decides lifetime |
| Result rule | "Confirmed" = 2 or 3 of 3 replicates + clean blank; "possible" = 1 of 3; "not detected" ≠ absent | Single detections are unreliable |
| Sample ID | CK-YYYYMMDD-SITE-SEAT (e.g. CK-20261206-AQ01-S3); the blank is CK-YYYYMMDD-BLANK-S1. Printed as QR labels on the day of the mission | Links lab result to GPS and sensor log |
| Battery | 3S 18650, 3,000 mAh: approx. 32 Wh, 26 Wh usable (80%) | Datasheet nominal 3.6 V |
| Energy per mission | approx. 12 Wh (stepper driver switched off between moves) | Pump is the main load |
| Missions per charge | approx. **2** (about 4 with a spare pack); target confirmed in battery test 1–5 Dec | 26 ÷ 12 |
| Pod cost | approx. AED 1,710 (47 BOM items); + AED 435 diorama = AED 2,145 | BOM |
| Pod cost at 100 units | approx. AED 1,150–1,350 | Bulk parts, one custom board; keep 3D printing |
| Commercial sampler price | Smith-Root kit: USD 7,495 (≈ AED 27,500) on a reseller page; ">€10,000" in a 2025 paper. Say "about USD 7,500–11,000" | Do NOT use "USD 1,000–5,000" |
| Lab cost per sample (metabarcoding) | approx. AED 260 (university core) to AED 1,375 (Sylphium, €320) | Part 3 lab table |
| Total cost per sample | approx. AED 270–1,390 (field consumables approx. AED 2, courier approx. AED 10) | Lab is most of it |
| qPCR (one species) | approx. AED 190–470 per sample | TAMUCC core to Sylphium €109 |
| Field kit weight | approx. 3.5 kg total; limit 5 kg | Carry test to confirm |
| Lab shipment | 13 filters: aquarium 6 (1 blank, 3 ethanol, 1 silica, 1 syringe) + dilution 3 + Green Planet 4 (1 blank, 3 reps) | Q154 |
