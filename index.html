01 / SHED

Animals leave DNA everywhere

Skin, hair and saliva rub off on leaves high in the canopy — monkeys, sloths, frogs.

02 / RAIN

Rain washes it down

Every shower carries that DNA off the leaves and into the forest streams.

03 / CATCH

We filter the water

The Pod pumps up to 1 litre through a 0.45 µm filter — finer than a hair.

04 / KEEP

Preserved and logged

2 mL of ethanol keeps the DNA safe inside numbered cartridge CK-T007, with the place, time and volume.

05 / READ

A lab reads the guest list

DNA barcodes are matched to species. In French Guiana, canopy rain revealed 562 kinds of life.

01

Self-check

Battery, sensors, GPS. The carousel finds seat 1.

02

Index

A clean cartridge turns under the nozzle. Stake the Pod, or wait for rain.

03

Test

Murky water? Take a smaller sample so the filter won’t clog.

04

Prime

Flush the hose with site water, to waste.

05

Filter

The nozzle seals and up to 1 litre goes through. Rising pump current means a clog, so it stops.

06

Preserve

Drain, then 2 mL of ethanol onto the filter.

07

Log

Cartridge, seat, GPS, time, volume, turbidity, temperature.

08

Rinse + blank

Nozzle lifts, the hose is flushed. One bottled-water blank per mission proves nothing leaked.
