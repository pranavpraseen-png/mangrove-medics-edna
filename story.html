01 / SHED

Animals leave DNA everywhere

Skin, hair and saliva rub off on leaves high in the canopy — monkeys, sloths, frogs.

02 / RAIN

Rain washes it down

Every shower carries that DNA off the leaves and into the streams. In French Guiana, canopy rain revealed 562 kinds of life.

03 / CATCH

We filter the water

The Pod pulls up to 1 litre through a 0.45 µm filter — finer than a hair. If the filter clogs it stops early; 250 mL is the minimum that counts.

04 / KEEP

Preserved and logged

≥95% ethanol keeps the DNA safe. A QR label like CK-20261206-AQ01-S3 links the filter to its place, time and volume.

05 / READ

A lab reads the guest list

DNA barcodes are matched to species. We call a species “confirmed” only when 2 of 3 replicates agree and the blank is clean.

The Pod, taken apart

About 2.5 kg, carried by hand to the stream bank. Wet parts sit low over a drip tray; the electronics stay in a sealed dry zone with silica gel and a vent.

01

Self-check

Battery, sensors, GPS and clock. The carousel finds seat 1 by its double magnet.

02

Field blank

Seat 1 always goes first: 1 L of distilled water through the whole water path. A clean blank proves nothing leaked in.

03

Clean the site

New bagged intake tube and funnel. Bleach the nozzle for 10 minutes, rinse. Then flush 250 mL of site water through empty seat 8.

04

Test

Turbidity check. Rising or very muddy water (over about 300 NTU) means no sampling today.

05

Filter

Water is pulled through the filter, then the pump. Stops at 1 L, 20 minutes or flow under 20 mL/min. Counts only if ≥250 mL.

06

Preserve

≥95% ethanol onto the filter, then the cartridge is capped.

07

Log

QR sample ID, seat, GPS, time, weighed volume, turbidity, temperature.

08

Repeat

3 replicates per site, 2 sites: 6 real samples per mission. Cartridges are bleached and reloaded at school.

1 field blank, 6 real samples, 1 empty flush seat. Never “8 samples”.

Water per filter1 L

Target. Stops at 1 L, 20 minutes or flow under 20 mL/min. A sample counts only if ≥250 mL went through.

Water pathPull-through

Intake → cartridge (filter) → pump → waste. The pump only sees filtered water, and a clog can’t burst a tube.

Preservation≥95%

Ethanol on every filter, shipped with it. Longmire’s buffer if the lab asks. Silica gel is tested side by side on 6 Dec.

Battery≈ 2

Missions per charge (approx. 12 Wh each from 26 Wh usable); about 4 with a spare pack. Battery test 1–5 Dec.

Cost≈ AED 1,710

For our Pod (47 parts). Commercial eDNA samplers cost about USD 7,500–11,000.

Between sites

New bagged, pre-bleached intake tube and funnel. 10% bleach on the nozzle for 10 minutes, then rinse. 250 mL of site water flushed through seat 8 before the first sample.

04 · TESTING

Built now. Proven by December.

The prototype is being built. Nothing has been lab-tested yet — this is our test plan, with what “success” means for each step.

  1. by 8 Nov
    Bleach durability + flow calibration

    20 bleach-and-rinse cycles on 2 cartridges; 5 timed runs to know the real flow rate (±10%).

  2. by 15 Nov
    Usability, lab and tank locked

    5 classmates run a mission from the picture guide. Lab partner, price and aquarium chosen.

  3. 20–25 Nov
    Waterproofing

    Sealed box with tissue inside, 30 minutes in a bucket, 3 times. Tissue must stay dry.

  4. 28 Nov–3 Dec
    Dye leak and carry-over

    8 runs with coloured water. No dye may reach the next cartridge.

  5. 1–5 Dec
    Battery life

    Full missions in a loop with current logged. Goal: at least 2 missions per charge.

  6. 6 Dec
    Aquarium test

    Tank with known fish: blank + 3 Pod replicates, a dilution series and silica vs ethanol.

  7. 10 Dec
    The Green Planet Dubai

    Indoor rainforest: blank + 3 replicates, compared with their animal list.

  8. by 15 Dec
    Ship 13 filters to the lab

    With the sample manifest and the chain-of-custody form.
