# BIOGLOW Robot Game: missions and plan (Founders Edition, SPIKE)

Match is 2 min 30 s. Scores come from the official rulebook (Spanish translation, checked against FLLCasts). Numbers with * were counted from the rulebook pictures: **check every number against the English rulebook on the mat before you plan runs.**

| # | Mission | What to do | Max | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Drone Survey | Launch the drone off the mat (20); +10 LiDAR map flipped and scanner marker in the survey area. No equipment touching. | 30 | Medium |
| M02 | Exploding Seeds | Release seeds off the stalk, 10 each | ~30* | Easy |
| M03 | Flip the Rock | Exploration flag down (20); +10 rock back at start | 30 | Medium |
| M04 | Lucky Leaves | Leaf 1 off without touching the nest (10), leaf 2 +20; grasshopper must stay put | 30 | Hard |
| M05 | Reaching Roots | Root partly (10) or fully (20) extended. No equipment touching. | 20 | Easy |
| M06 | Leafcutter Frenzy | Ant base touching the nest with leaf pieces, 10 each | ~30* | Medium |
| M07 | Humongous Fungus | Mycelium fully out (20); +10 if it meets the other team's root | 30 | Easy/Medium |
| M08 | Tangled | Vine touching the mat | 30 | Medium |
| M09 | Research Platform | Tower secured (10), camera trap out (10), seed off the tree (10) | 30 | Medium |
| M10 | Fragile Microhabitats | Spider and snail habitats untouched, 10 each | 20 | Easy (plan paths) |
| M11 | Window to the Past | Root cover down on the mat | 20 | Easy |
| M12 | Forest Elder | Column up touching the tree (20), strap round the post (10) | 30 | Hard |
| M13 | Keystone Species | Your own 20-brick keystone model on the platform with saplings raised | 30 | Medium |
| M14 | Seeds of Renewal | Seeds in the replanting station 5 each, +5 each touching the mat | 10/seed* | Medium |
| M15 | Biocentric Architecture | Roof up, dome in, compost lid open (10 each); +10 for the zone's main need | 40 | Medium/Hard |

**Free points:** equipment inspection (20) if everything fits in one launch area under 305 mm tall, and precision tokens (up to 50 if you interrupt the robot at most once outside home).

**Rules to remember:** home is split left/right (max 2 technicians per side, no swapping, nothing passed between sides). Max 4 motors. Sensors: touch/force, colour, distance, gyro only.

## Suggested order to build
1. Design every attachment to fit one launch area (+20) and keep runs reliable (+50 tokens). 70 points before any mission.
2. M05 + M07 together (50), short pushes near home.
3. M11 + M10 (40): one push, and a path that avoids the habitats.
4. M08 Tangled (30) with one good hook attachment.
5. M02 then M14: release the seeds, then deliver them.
6. M01 and M13 next. Leave M04, M12 and the M15 bonus for later in the season.
